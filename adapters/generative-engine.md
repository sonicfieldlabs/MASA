# Generative engine adapter archetype

Placeholder namespace: `engine:`

A generative audio engine maps its cultivation, synthesis, or breeding operations through the Generation and Transformation profiles while retaining its domain vocabulary as application terms. Domain taxonomies — for example a biological-compositional family of cells, strains, tissues, organisms, and families — stay in the engine's namespace and are not MASA Core classes.

The adapter should retain prompt and conditioning provenance, parent material, method, seed state, tool/model/provider identity, selection and rejection, policy state, and multiparent graft lineage. Failed or refused provider operations remain receipts without invented descendants.

Family resemblance does not establish shared bytes, authorship, ownership, or consent.
