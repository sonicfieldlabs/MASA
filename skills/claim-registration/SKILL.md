# Claim registration

1. Choose exactly one claim kind: heard, measured, inferred, interpreted, speculative, or undetermined.
2. Name the actor, subject, evidence basis, method, creation time, uncertainty, confidence state, and disclosure.
3. For heard claims, reference a listening pass and its aperture.
4. For measured claims, supply a value, unit, method, and window or range.
5. For inferred claims, supply evidence and the named rule, comparison, or model.
6. For interpreted claims, declare the position or contextual frame.
7. For speculative claims, state the rationale and boundary from observation.
8. For undetermined claims, name the reason without substituting a fabricated value.
9. Preserve contradictory or incomparable claims as separate attributed accounts.

Never infer confidence from actor type, source freshness, system health, or rhetorical certainty.
